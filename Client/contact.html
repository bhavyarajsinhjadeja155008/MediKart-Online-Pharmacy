About MediKart

Healthcare made simple since 2020

MediKart started as a small neighbourhood chemist shop in Rajkot. Seeing how difficult it was for elderly people and working families to visit a pharmacy every month, we decided to bring the pharmacy online. Today we serve customers across Gujarat with the same personal care.

Every order is checked by a registered pharmacist before it is packed. Prescription medicines are sold only after a valid prescription is verified. We buy directly from licensed distributors, so every product is genuine and stored at the correct temperature.

Our mission is to make medicines affordable and easy to reach. We offer fair prices, free delivery on bigger orders, refill reminders and free advice from our pharmacists on call.
